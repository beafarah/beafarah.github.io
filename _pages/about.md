I am a post-doctoral researcher at the Department of Mathematical Sciences, University of Copenhagen. 

I am currently working with [Carsten Wiuf](https://web.math.ku.dk/~pbx512/) on developing statistical methods to infer genetic relationships among wild and domestic cattle species (genus Bos).

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Applied Mathematics from the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro and a master’s degree in Data Science, Statistics, and Machine Learning from ENSAE Paris, funded by an Eiffel Excellence Scholarship.

Between 2023 and 2026, I carried out my PhD on survival analysis under the supervision of [Olivier Bouaziz](https://obouaziz.github.io/) and [Aurélien Latouche](https://curie.fr/personne/aurelien-latouche).  
This work was conducted at MAP 5, Université Paris Cité, and Inserm U1331, Institut Curie, and focused on designing clinical trials using quantiles of survival time.

My research interests include statistical applications to the life sciences and medicine.
